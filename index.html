Voice Email Caller

A personal, single-user email alerting service

Voice Email Caller watches its operator's own email inbox for mail from a configured list of senders and alerts him by telephone call, falling back to SMS if the call cannot be completed.

It is operated by one individual for his own use. It is not a commercial product, has no public sign-up, and sends messages to exactly one phone number — the operator's own.

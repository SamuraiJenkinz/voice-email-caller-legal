How Opt-In Works

Voice Email Caller · Last updated 24 August 2026

Summary for reviewers. This messaging program has exactly one recipient: the operator of the service, who is also the Twilio account holder. There is no public sign-up, no customer list, and no mechanism by which any other person's number could enter the system. Consent is given by the operator to himself, by configuring his own number.

Who sends, who receives, and why

SenderKevin Taylor, an individual, from his own Twilio long code.
RecipientKevin Taylor — his own mobile handset. One number, permanently. Nobody else.
WhyThe service watches his own email inbox for messages from a short, self-chosen list of senders and telephones him to read them aloud. If that call cannot be completed, an SMS delivers the same alert so time-sensitive mail is not missed.
VolumeFewer than 10 messages per week, frequently zero.

The opt-in step, concretely

There is no web form, no keyword, and no advertisement — because there is no audience to advertise to. The only way a phone number can enter this system is for the operator to type it into a configuration file on his own computer during setup: 

# .env — local configuration, on the operator's own machine
OWNER_PHONE_NUMBER=+1XXXXXXXXXX     <-- the operator enters his own mobile here
TWILIO_FROM_NUMBER=+1XXXXXXXXXX     <-- the number he purchased

Entering that value is the act of consent. It is performed by the account holder, on his own hardware, for his own handset. The application reads exactly one destination number from that configuration and has no code path that would message any other number.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all further messages. Reply HELP for help. Opt-out is honoured immediately and independently by the underlying carrier. The operator may also revoke consent by deleting the number from the configuration, which stops messages at the source.

Sample messages

Recipients receive messages of exactly these kinds:

Voice Email Caller: new email from [sender@example.com], subject
"[Email subject]". Your alert call could not be completed, so the
message is included here: [Full email body text, which may contain
links such as https://example.com/article]. Reply HELP for help,
STOP to opt out.

Voice Email Caller: alert call to your mobile failed after [3]
attempts at [HH:MM] on [MM/DD/YYYY]. Email from [sender@example.com],
subject "[Email subject]". Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP
for help, STOP to opt out.

Because the message relays the operator's own received email verbatim, the body may contain links or phone numbers originating from the sender of that email. This is declared on the campaign. Message and data rates may apply.

What this program is not

  • Not marketing, promotional, or advertising messaging.
  • Not sent to customers, subscribers, leads, or any third party.
  • No phone number is bought, sold, rented, shared, or collected from anyone.
  • No affiliate, lead-generation, or high-risk content of any kind.

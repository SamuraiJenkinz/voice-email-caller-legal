Privacy Policy

Voice Email Caller · Last updated 24 August 2026

What this service is

Voice Email Caller is a personal, single-user notification service operated by an individual for his own use. It monitors the operator's own email inbox and alerts him by phone call, with an SMS fallback if the call cannot be completed. It is not a commercial product, has no public sign-up, and has no users other than the operator himself.

Whose phone number we hold

Exactly one: the operator's own mobile number, which he enters into a configuration file on his own machine during setup. No phone numbers are collected from anyone else, because the service has no mechanism by which anyone else could provide one.

Mobile numbers are never shared. No mobile information is sold, rented, traded, or shared with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile opt-in data or consent records are shared with third parties. Phone numbers are transmitted only to the telephony provider (Twilio) strictly for the purpose of placing the call or delivering the message, which is the sole technical means of delivery.

Message frequency

Messages are sent only when new email arrives from a sender on the operator's own configured watchlist and the alerting phone call could not be completed. Expected frequency is fewer than 10 messages per week. Frequency varies with incoming mail and may be zero in a given week.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out and receive no further messages. Reply HELP for help. Opt-out takes effect immediately and is honoured by the underlying telephony provider independently of this service.

What data the service handles

  • Email content — read from the operator's own mailbox via the Gmail API, using credentials he authorised himself, in order to read the message aloud or include it in a fallback SMS. Content is processed transiently for delivery and is not published or sold.
  • The operator's mobile number — stored locally in the operator's own configuration and passed to the telephony provider to place calls and send messages.
  • Operational records — minimal local state (such as which messages have already been delivered) so that a restart does not cause a duplicate alert. Stored locally.

Third parties

The service relies on Google (Gmail API) for mail access and Twilio for telephony. Data reaches those providers only as required to operate the service, and is governed by their respective privacy policies. No other third party receives data.

Retention

Email content is not retained beyond what is needed to deliver a given alert. Local operational state persists only to prevent duplicate alerts and may be deleted at any time by the operator.

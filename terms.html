Voice Email Caller · Last updated 24 August 2026

1. The service

Voice Email Caller ("the service") is a personal notification tool operated by an individual for his own use. It watches the operator's own email inbox for mail from a configured list of senders and alerts him by telephone call, falling back to SMS if the call cannot be completed. It is not offered to the public, is not sold, and has no users other than the operator.

2. Who receives messages

Only the operator. The service messages exactly one number — the operator's own mobile, entered by him into a local configuration file on his own machine. There is no sign-up mechanism through which any other person could subscribe.

3. Consent

Consent is given by the operator when he configures his own mobile number for use with the service. That act constitutes his express consent to receive alert messages at that number.

4. Message frequency and charges

Messages are sent only on receipt of watched email where the alerting call could not be completed. Expected frequency is fewer than 10 messages per week and may be zero. Message and data rates may apply.

5. Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Opt-out requests are honoured immediately.

6. Acceptable use

The service is used solely to relay the operator's own incoming email to himself. It is not used to send marketing, promotional, solicited or unsolicited bulk messaging, or any message to a third party.

It depends on third-party services (Google and Twilio) and on the operator's own hardware and network, any of which may fail. It must not be relied upon for emergency, safety-critical, medical, or time-critical notification.
